The collection

We started with one wig and a lot of questions.

Belevish Hair began when a friend wanted a wig that looked natural, felt light and didn’t cost a month’s pay. We couldn’t find one, so we set out to make it.

Every style in our collection is tested on real heads before it’s sold. We check the hairline, the weight of the cap and how the hair holds a curl after a week of wear. If we wouldn’t wear it ourselves, it doesn’t ship.

We’re a small team and we answer every message. Tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll help you pick.

Size guide

Measure around your head, from the front hairline, behind the ears and across the nape. Use a soft tape and keep it snug, not tight.

Size Head circumference
Small 20.5–21.5 in
Medium 21.5–22.5 in

Between sizes? Pick the larger one and tighten the adjustable band.

How do I care for my wig?

Wash every 6–8 wears with sulfate-free shampoo, air dry on a stand and detangle from the ends up.

Can I colour or heat style it?

Yes, up to 180°C. For colouring, book our custom service so we can match the shade safely.

How long does a wig last?

With good care, 6 to 12 months of regular wear.
